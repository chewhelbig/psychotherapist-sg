Getting Here

Havelock MRT Exit 2 is about seven minutes on foot. Outram Park MRT Exit 7 or 8 is about thirteen.

The nearest bus stop is Blk 55 (06051), roughly three minutes away. Services 5, 16, 33, 63, 121, 122, 123, 195 and 851 connect to Raffles Place and Shenton Way, Outram, HarbourFront, Orchard, Bukit Merah, Bedok, Pasir Ris, Yishun, and Sentosa via 123.

Finding the Room

Arrive a little early to locate the building. Look for two pink chairs and walk up to the second floor. My door will be open five minutes before the appointment.
